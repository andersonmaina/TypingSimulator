# Human Typing Simulator

A small tool that types text for you into whatever field you click into,
at a human-like pace with natural pauses and the occasional typo.

## Install - Windows only LOL 💨  ^_~

1. Clone repo
2. Double-click `TypingSimulator.exe` to launch it. No installation needed.

> Note: Windows SmartScreen may warn you about running an unrecognized
> app the first time. Click **More info -> Run anyway**.

## How to use

1. Click into the text box and type or paste the text you want typed out.
2. (Optional) Adjust the sliders:
   - **Speed** — how fast it types, in words per minute.
   - **Typo chance** — how often it makes and corrects a small typo.
   - **Random pause chance** — how often it pauses briefly, like a person thinking.
   - **Countdown** — how many seconds you get to click into the target field before typing starts.
3. Click **Start**.
4. During the countdown, click into the field where you want the text typed (a browser, chat box, document, etc.).
5. Sit back — the app types the text there automatically.
6. To cancel at any time, click **Stop** or press **Esc**.

## Other controls

- **Clear** — empties the text box.
- **▲ / ▼** — step backward/forward through text you've previously run, so you can reuse or re-check an earlier entry without retyping it.

## Notes

- Keep the app window open while it's typing — closing it mid-type will stop the simulation.
- If you move your mouse to a screen corner during typing, some systems may trigger a failsafe stop — just click back into your target field and press Start again.
